Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 24, 2026 • Last Updated: May 24, 2026

Welcome to Apex Partner Group ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate as an authorized channel partner for various real estate developers and projects. We are deeply committed to protecting your personal privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled securely and transparently.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website apexpartnergroup.com, use our landing pages, or interact with our digital advertisements and sales representatives.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when expressing interest in obtaining information about our services or the real estate projects we promote.

  • Identity & Contact Data: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Property Preferences: Your budget range, preferred location, configuration choice (e.g., 2 BHK, 3 BHK, villas), and timeline for purchasing.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and usage data collected via cookies and tracking pixels when you visit our site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data specifically to streamline your property search and coordinate with developers. This includes:

  • Responding to your inquiries and scheduling site visits.
  • Sharing project brochures, price sheets, and construction updates.
  • Verifying your eligibility for specific developer offers or payment plans.
  • Sending marketing materials, newsletters, or promotional offers (which you can opt-out of at any time).

3. How We Share Your Information

As a channel partner, a core part of our service involves connecting you with real estate developers. We share your data under the following circumstances:

  • With Real Estate Developers: Once you express a definitive interest in a specific project, we share your contact and preference details with the respective developer to facilitate site visits, official bookings, and direct communication.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted third-party vendors (like CRM platforms, email marketing services, or SMS gateways) who assist us in running our business.
  • Legal Requirements: We will disclose information if required to do so by law, regulatory authorities (such as real estate regulatory bodies), or to protect our legal rights.

Important Note: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies unrelated to your real estate inquiry.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We will retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, or as required to comply with legal, tax, or regulatory requirements.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • The right to request the deletion of your data from our systems.
  • The right to opt-out of receiving marketing communications from us.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
